Commands use explicit staging/production profiles; never guess an account, tenant, bucket or Snowflake role. ## Safe diagnostic command shapes

The implementation supplies a read-only `bridge-admin` CLI with explicit environment and scope, bound to short-lived operator identity. FND-005/OPS-010 must test these interfaces before documentation claims they exist.

```bash
aws sts get-caller-identity --profile bridge-staging-readonly
bridge-admin health account --env staging --tenant TENANT_UUID --account ACCOUNT_UUID
bridge-admin batch inspect --env staging --batch BATCH_UUID
bridge-admin coverage diff --env staging --tenant TENANT_UUID --source QUERY_HISTORY
bridge-admin reconciliation explain --env staging --run RECONCILIATION_UUID
```

Recovery commands require a scoped dry-run manifest containing affected partitions, expected counts/cost deltas, lease generation and rollback reference. An operator reviews this concrete manifest before executing a destructive or production-sensitive action. Reversible retries already authorized by an incident procedure do not need repeated approval.

## Incident and support protocol

SEV1: suspected disclosure, financial corruption broadly served, or total production outage. Page incident commander immediately; initial acknowledgment target15min during contracted coverage. (amended 2026-09-28, D-37) Coverage at launch is EU business hours (Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 CET); outside those hours SEV1 is paged on a best-effort basis (target acknowledgment 60 min); SEV2 and SEV3 wait for business hours. SEV2: major tenant blocked or large coverage backlog; target1h acknowledgment during coverage. SEV3: limited defect with workaround; next-business-day target. These are internal starting targets until staffing and customer contract approve actual commitments. Record impact and update cadence; never promise24×7 response without a staffed rota.

Support intake asks for tenant/request ID, UTC time, page/action, expected behavior and safe screenshot; never asks for passwords, key pairs or unrestricted raw SQL. Customer-facing updates state known impact, workaround and next update time, without speculative cause. Every incident closes with verified recovery, owner, evidence and follow-up tasks.
