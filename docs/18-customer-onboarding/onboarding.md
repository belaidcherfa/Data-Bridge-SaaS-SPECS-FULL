# Customer onboarding and first-value acceptance

Canonical domain contract. Owner: Customer success / Snowflake / FinOps. Implementation state: NOT_STARTED.


## Onboarding state and first value

Onboarding is a resumable control-plane workflow: Bridge organization → Snowflake organization WIF → account discovery/selection → per-account WIF → capability verification → requested/available history plan → extraction/S3/Snowpipe/dbt progress → reconciliation → ownership → allocation simulation → budget → monitors → actionable insight review. Reports and user invitations complete the first-value pack. A connection alone never marks onboarding complete. (amended 2026-09-28, D-38, G-ONB-05) The wizard is **fully usable without Bridge staff** (PRD §158), and the first customer additionally receives a Bridge-led first-value workshop (ONB-005); every operator action on a tenant during onboarding is recorded as a manual intervention (reason, step, ops call) and becomes a product backlog item, and a clean-room rehearsal on synthetic accounts proves the unassisted path (ONB-002). (amended 2026-09-28, D-29) After capability verification, **steady-state synchronization starts immediately** and the historical backfill runs in a fair background lane; coverage merges contiguously, there is no separate catch-up phase (refines PRD §40), and fresh data appears on day 1.

Each step stores configuration version, actor, prerequisite evidence, started/completed times and blocked reason. No analytical totals are duplicated into PostgreSQL; status references the accepted Snowflake publication. (amended 2026-09-28, G-ONB-01, G-ONB-07) Step status is a **projection of domain facts** (connection and capability state, backfill plan and coverage, reconciliation run, ruleset/simulation, budget/monitor/report, insight disposition) plus an append-only acknowledgement ledger for human decisions bound to the exact domain revision acknowledged, so a revoked account can never leave a step marked COMPLETED. Blockers have stable codes, detection rules and customer messages (for example PrivateLink-only account `CON_PRIVATELINK_R2`, network policy blocking the service user, reseller without billing access, standalone account without organization views) — state machine and blocker catalog in [ONB backlog](../22-implementation-readiness/backlog/ONB.md) §3. Browser closure, worker restart or repeated submission resumes the same operation. The customer sees required versus optional grants, actual source-retained history, requested365-day target, snapshot-only limits and excluded/unpriced spend before consent to start. A standalone-account connection is supported with organization coverage explicitly limited. (amended 2026-09-28, D-08, D-09, D-23, D-11) Before consent the customer also sees: the processing region (single EU deployment); Bridge's egress IPs and the optional network-policy block of the install script; the dedicated `BRIDGE_FINOPS_WH` warehouse, its resource-monitor quota and the estimated monthly credits for steady state and for the one-off backfill (365 days of query-level detail including sanitized text, so the backfill estimate is larger than for metadata only; ONB-101 computes duration and credit ranges from measured coefficients and binds consent to the estimate version); that QUERY_HISTORY access (GOVERNANCE_VIEWER) exposes query text, which is sanitized before storage. (amended 2026-09-28, D-35, D-20) A Snowflake **trial account** may be connected for demonstrations only: all figures are labelled as demonstration data and the account is excluded from paid entitlements, reconciliation claims, close and chargeback. All editions, organization forms and contract types are supported in R1, with capability limits disclosed.

Historical completion means contiguous coverage of every selected required source's available authorized range, with missing and unsupported ranges disclosed and accepted. A successful extraction counter is not proof of transformed or reconciled data. Progress separates source extraction, journal, Snowpipe, transformation and financial validation; use counts/dates, not invented percentages. Estimate completion only when throughput evidence exists. (amended 2026-09-28, G-ONB-04) A per-source coverage ratio (covered days ÷ available days) is a measured value and is allowed; there is no blended cross-source percentage (refines the PRD §120 "Estimated coverage 96%" example); an ETA is shown only as a p50–p90 range after at least three completed chunks.

(amended 2026-09-28, G-ONB-02, G-ONB-03) **Realistic first-value timeline.** First value is split. **FV-1** (weeks 1–4): cost by account, service and day over retained history; warehouse query-attributed versus idle; top query families; storage trend; Data Health with gaps; reconciliation of the latest billing-stable month (the latest month M with month end + 5 days + 72 h ≤ now) against Snowflake currency data, plus the invoice or usage statement when supplied; Bridge overhead; a retroactive allocation simulation labelled "current tag state applied to past periods"; a budget with forecast, a monitor dry run, one report; an insight review and optional action with frozen baseline. **FV-2** (first full calendar month after enrollment, closed at month end + 5 days): a Bridge-closed period, tag-faithful showback/chargeback statement, a full month of budget versus actual, live monitor incidents, and a verified saving only if the action is at least 14 complete days old. Typical elapsed time: customer procurement, DPA and security questionnaire 5–20 business days; customer change approval and network allowlisting 2–10 business days; organization and account scripts 0.5–2 days; probes and consent 0.5 day; finance-first backfill hours and query-grain backfill days (measured by OPS-105); FV-1 acceptance ≈ 3–4 weeks after the scripts run; FV-2 ≈ 5–9 weeks after. ONB-005 accepts FV-1; LCH-004 reviews FV-2.

## Customer acceptance record

Use [first-value checklist](FIRST_VALUE.md). Record tenant/org/account locators safely, selected scopes, retained history boundaries, capability exceptions, reconciliation period/currency/basis, allocation method, accountable owners, first budget/monitor/report, reviewed insight and support contacts. Customer approval of a disclosed limitation does not change an unavailable metric into a measured result or a failed reconciliation into RECONCILED.

Installation is executed by the customer's authorized Snowflake administrator with reviewed least-privilege scripts. Bridge never requests ACCOUNTADMIN as its ongoing runtime role. (amended 2026-09-28, G-CON-05) The administrator runs the marker-guarded install script once with a user able to use ACCOUNTADMIN (resource monitor and SNOWFLAKE database-role grants need it); re-running converges the existing Bridge objects. Offboarding pauses schedules, disables identities, exports approved records, applies retention/deletion policy and verifies access revocation. Reconnection uses the same tenant/account identity mapping and versioned capabilities; do not create duplicate historical charges. (amended 2026-09-28, G-ONB-06, U-11) ONB-102 owns the offboarding request, export bundle (an API-102 export kind) and reconnection re-binding by organization and account locator; deletion runs through the OPS-005 privacy-request workflow and the OPS-104 tombstone log.
