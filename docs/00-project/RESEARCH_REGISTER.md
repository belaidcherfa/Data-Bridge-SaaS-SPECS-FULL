## Activation evidence

For each source record official URL/retrieval date, actual account/edition/region, exact SELECT projection, returned types/nullability, required grants, natural key, time semantics, observed latency, requested/available history, live positive/negative fixture and contract version. If official availability differs from the design, quarantine the source or amend its contract/ADR with evidence; do not silently approximate financial facts. Source catalog entries without a fully verified projection are deliberately activation-gated, not permission to invent fields.
