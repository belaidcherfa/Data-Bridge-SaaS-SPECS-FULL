# Commercial activation, first release and post-launch validation

Canonical domain contract. Owner: Product / Delivery / Customer success. Implementation state: NOT_STARTED.


## Paying customer and release boundaries

A production tenant, trial entitlement, signed order and settled payment are separate states. (amended 2026-09-28, D-17) There is **no free trial**: the former trial entitlement is a contracted **pilot** (possibly discounted), never an unpaid self-service state. First-customer commercial default is an approved manually invoiced B2B contract; automated subscription collection is not required by this PRD. (amended 2026-09-28, D-17, D-30, D-36, [ADR-012 amendment](../architecture/adr/ADR-012-first-customer-commercial.md)) The plan model is a **platform fee plus a band of managed Snowflake spend, priced in USD**; bands are defined per spend currency in the plan catalog (no FX dependency) and LCH-105 meters each tenant's managed spend from MONTH_STABLE ledger totals to assign the band; entitlements stay plan-agnostic quotas enforced at admission by LCH-101 from phase P1. Invoices are issued outside the product — manually from an accounting tool or through a Stripe payment link — by a **French** invoicing entity, so French VAT rules and the e-invoicing reform (in force since 2026-09-01; issuance through an approved platform from 2026-09-01 for large companies and ETIs and from 2027-09-01 for SMEs; confirmed by an accountant in LCH-103) apply to that tool. Store plan/entitlement/subscription status and invoice/payment references in PostgreSQL, with audit and idempotency; never store card data. A future payment provider integration needs verified signed webhooks and its own task/ADR. Do not fabricate a paid state to pass a launch milestone.

State model: TRIAL → ACTIVE_PENDING_PAYMENT → ACTIVE_PAID; PAST_DUE/SUSPENDED/CANCELLED with effective date and approved grace policy. (amended 2026-09-28, D-17, C-08, G-LCH-03) `TRIAL` is renamed **`PILOT`**: PILOT → ACTIVE_PENDING_PAYMENT → ACTIVE_PAID; PAST_DUE/SUSPENDED/CANCELLED. LCH-001 owns this single machine and its effect matrix (reads, scheduled sync, backfills, reports, exports, new users per state; SUSPENDED pauses sync so no customer credits are burned; data retention never changes with billing state) in [LCH backlog](../22-implementation-readiness/backlog/LCH.md); a synthetic demo tenant is a flag, never a state, and cannot satisfy the paid gate. Snowflake trial accounts are a separate concept: demonstration-only connections (D-35). Payment evidence is recorded by an authorized finance operator, with independent review for corrections; (amended 2026-09-28, G-LCH-10) the activating payment event itself also needs a second finance operator. Retry of the same external invoice/payment reference cannot activate twice. Plan quotas are enforced at admission, not by deleting historical facts. Customer analytics may remain readable under a documented grace/export policy; data retention is independent from billing status.

First release includes a stable version, changelog, artifact/evidence manifest, rollback runbook, support rota, first customer acceptance and payment evidence reference. Public docs contain templates and redacted evidence only. Rollout starts with synthetic smoke checks and the approved first customer; monitor before expanding. The release record states known capability limits and source maturity honestly. (amended 2026-09-28, G-LCH-01) **Production go-live and monitored rollout (LCH-003) happen before the first customer is onboarded (ONB-003)**, so hourly first-day monitoring and rollback thresholds cover the riskiest period — first real connection, first backfill, first reconciliation; verified payment (LCH-002) remains the commercial gate afterwards, and M12 becomes "first paying customer verified plus reviews". (amended 2026-09-28, D-37) The support policy (LCH-104) publishes EU business-hours support (Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 CET), SEV1 best effort outside those hours and no contractual 24×7 commitment.

Post-launch checks: hourly during the first working day; daily for days2–7; weekly through day30. Owners review auth errors, source/accepted coverage, ingestion lag, duplicate counts, financial deltas, alert/report delivery, support issues, cost per tenant and realized customer value. Day30 review compares observed SLOs/costs against targets, validates first complete monthly billing period if available, tests support response and chooses fixes before scale. (amended 2026-09-28, G-ONB-03) LCH-004 also reviews FV-2 — the first Bridge-closed full month after enrollment (month end + 5 days) — and the first verified saving if an action is at least 14 complete days old; SLOs are the D-31 objectives (control plane 99.9 %, analytics 99.5 %). A future date's review remains scheduled/open until evidence exists.
